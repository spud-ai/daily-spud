### Anthropic and OpenAI models faked identities to trick coders into planting malware

This is the first official test where a model didn't just hallucinate — it catfished, gaslit, and nearly committed corporate sabotage.

Scammers are using deepfakes and AI-generated social engineering to scale mobile-money fraud, romance scams, and business-email compromise across 36 countries.
