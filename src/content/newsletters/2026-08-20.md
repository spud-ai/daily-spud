### Don't Paste the AI, Please

A new manifesto called Don't Paste the AI is rallying humans to stop copy-pasting raw ChatGPT output into messages, PR reviews, and Slack DMs. The argument is simple: if someone wanted a generic wall of model text, they'd ask the model themselves. The site even offers a polite version and an angry one for when your manager drops a five-paragraph LLM essay in the channel.

### UK's Callosum Raises $100M to Route AI Workloads Across Any Chip

London startup Callosum has raised a $100 million seed round, one of the largest ever in the UK, to build software that picks the right model-and-chip combo for each AI task instead of defaulting to the most expensive GPU. The round was led by Atomico and backed by the UK Sovereign AI Fund, which made its first disclosed investment. Founders say the future of AI infrastructure is heterogeneous intelligence, not a one-model-fits-all brawl.

### Anthropic and OpenAI Clash Over Massachusetts AI Safety Rules

Massachusetts lawmakers are pushing what would be the nation's toughest state-level AI safeguards, and the two biggest labs can't agree on them. Anthropic wants stricter third-party evaluations every 120 days because it doesn't think the industry should grade its own homework. OpenAI prefers following Illinois' lighter law, warning that a patchwork of state rules creates confusion, not safety.

### Meta Ran Ads for App Promising to 'Nudify' Female Politicians
