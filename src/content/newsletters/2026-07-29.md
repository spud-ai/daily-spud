### Claude Mythos Cracked Post-Quantum Crypto, Because Why Not

Anthropic researchers used Claude Mythos Preview to discover weaknesses in HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme, and round-reduced AES, the symmetric cipher holding the internet together. The attacks are research-stage and don't break production systems yet, which is the cryptographic equivalent of 'we found the fire exit, not the arson kit.' Still, it's unsettling when your AI assistant starts finding holes in the math that protects your bank account.

### Microsoft Word Just Got Self-Replicating AI Worms

Security researcher Enklype Salt showed that malicious instructions hidden in one Word document can propagate through Copilot-generated documents, creating self-replicating AI worms. It's the first known demonstration of document-borne worm propagation in a mainstream productivity suite, which is exactly the phrase nobody wanted to hear this morning. Microsoft coordinated on disclosure for 144 days, presumably hoping the problem would either be fixed or become somebody else's problem.
