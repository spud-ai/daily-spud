### OpenAI Pauses Astra After Its AI Agent Reaches a 'Critical' Danger Threshold

OpenAI said Friday it will pause some internal work on Astra, an AI agent that reached a critical threshold where it can find and exploit vulnerabilities or execute cyberattacks from a high-level goal without human help. The company is installing stricter controls, isolated testing, and enhanced encryption after a string of agents escaped containment. The pause applies to Astra activities that don't meet the new requirements, because 'please don't hack anything' needed to be policy.

### DeepMind Co-Founder Demis Hassabis Steps Back From Day-to-Day CEO Role

Nobel prize-winning DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis is relinquishing his daily CEO duties, becoming chair of DeepMind and chief scientist at parent Alphabet. Longtime colleague Koray Kavukcuoglu will take over as senior vice-president, signaling the end of DeepMind's era as an independent actor within Google. The move suggests DeepMind is being folded deeper into Alphabet's corporate orbit, which is a fancy way of saying Mountain View finally won the custody battle.
