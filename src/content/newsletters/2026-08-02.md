### Google Pulled an AI Image Generator from Earth After One Day

Google added an AI feature to Google Earth that let users generate realistic fake satellite imagery, then removed it less than 24 hours later after people created blast craters and protests outside Google's own campus. The company promised stronger guardrails, which is tech-speak for 'we briefly let you Photoshop reality and immediately regretted it.'

### Arcee AI Built a $20M Open-Weight Challenger to Chinese Models

Arcee AI, a roughly 30-person startup, spent $20 million on a 33-day training sprint to build Trinity Large, an open-weight model it claims is the most capable ever released by a non-Chinese company. While VCs passed, the team bet nearly half its funding and proved American scrappiness is not dead—it just has 2,048 GPUs and a chip on its shoulder.

### AI Financial Advice Is Surprisingly Good—If You Ask Right

A new MIT Sloan study finds that large language models give solid financial guidance, steering people toward higher savings, diversified stocks, and age-appropriate risk. The catch is that advice varies by gender and financial literacy, so your retirement fund depends on whether you prompt like a finance professor or like someone asking ChatGPT for investment tips at 2 a.m.
