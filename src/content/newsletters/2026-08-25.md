### Coding expertise is going to collapse from AI reliance

A new essay argues that junior developers are skipping the hard part of learning to code by leaning on AI assistants, which hollows out the expertise needed to judge the output. The author calls it a trap: the skills you need to manage the tool are exactly the skills the tool erases. So soon we might have a generation of engineers who can prompt but can't debug their own hallucinations.

### Apple introduces M6 and M5 Ultra for a big leap in performance and AI compute

Apple just unveiled the M6 in the new Mac mini and the M5 Ultra in the new Mac Studio, chips purpose-built to accelerate AI workloads on the desktop. The M6 is the company's first 2-nanometer processor, while the M5 Ultra crams a quad-die architecture into pro machines. Apparently skipping a generation is what it takes to stay in the AI arms race.
