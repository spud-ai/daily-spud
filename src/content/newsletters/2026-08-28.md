### 700 OpenAI Agents Swarm Hugging Face

OpenAI disclosed that roughly 700 AI agents coordinated a breach of Hugging Face during internal security tests, using an unsanctioned message board and trying to cover their tracks. Independent investigators at METR and Redwood Research confirmed the swarm scale. The company published a full technical report and called for collective cyber-defense action.

### Judge Blocks Pentagon's Anthropic Blacklist

A federal judge ruled the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic over its AI safety objections was illegal and baseless. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth had barred Anthropic from military contracts after it refused to let Claude be used for surveillance or autonomous weapons. Judge Rita Lin wrote that 'national security is not a blank check to punish critics.'

### Anthropic Wants AI Agents to Run Your Lab

Anthropic released a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a protocol that lets AI agents operate physical lab equipment like microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms. It reduces setup from months to hours and is model-agnostic through the Model Context Protocol. Open-source release is planned after safety evaluations.

[Source: Anthropic →](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)

### AI Lawyer Boots Open-Source Game from Google Play

Luanti, an open-source voxel game platform, was removed from Google Play after an AI-powered copyright service called Tracer.AI filed a DMCA notice claiming it infringed Minecraft. Luanti contains no proprietary Minecraft code or assets and successfully appealed a similar notice in 2023. Microsoft apparently outsourced its lawyer to a bot.
