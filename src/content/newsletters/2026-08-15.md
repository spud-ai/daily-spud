### Google Open-Sources HEIR to Make Private AI Actually Private

Google launched HEIR, an open-source compiler that lets AI models run on fully encrypted data without decrypting it first. The demos span private recommendations, fraud detection, and network anomaly detection—useful for hospitals, banks, and anyone tired of surveillance capitalism. It’s still slower than normal inference, but now privacy is a cost problem, not a math problem.
