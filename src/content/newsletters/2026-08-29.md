### Debian Votes to Allow Generative AI, As Long As It Actually Works

The Debian project approved a general resolution saying it neither endorses nor prohibits generative AI, but every AI-assisted contribution must meet the same quality, legal, and maintainability standards as human work. It is the most polite way to say, 'You can use the robot, but you still have to clean up after it.' Finally, an open-source community brave enough to admit the issue isn't the tool—it's the pull request.

### Google's Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Finally Understands Your Mumbling

Google released Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a speech-to-text model designed to handle background noise, jargon, and disfluencies without turning your voice into a ransom note. It is already powering live transcription in the Gemini app and is now available to developers. Voice assistants have spent a decade pretending 'call Mom' means 'call Bob'; this is their first real hearing test.
