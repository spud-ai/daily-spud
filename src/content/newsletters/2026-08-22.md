### A California DA Let AI Proofread Criminal Cases. Errors Followed.

A former prosecutor claims her supervisor blocked her from immediately reporting the mistakes. Now a judge is weighing sanctions against the district attorney.

### AI Made Homework Look Great. Then Exams Happened.

A study of 27,000 Chinese students found that kids who used AI tools like DeepSeek and Doubao saw homework scores rise 18 percent and completion time drop by 30 percent. When those same students took exams without AI access, they scored 20 percent below classmates who had done the work themselves. Turns out outsourcing your thinking has downsides.

### Anti-AI Fonts Don't Stop Scrapers. They Stop Humans.

A new critique argues that fonts designed to confuse AI scrapers are fundamentally broken: they hurt accessibility by scrambling the text that screen readers parse, and any machine-readable metadata can be exploited by the very scrapers they're meant to block. The real fix isn't typography; it's figuring out who gets to read what, which is harder than making pretty glyphs.
