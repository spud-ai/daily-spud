### Microsoft's MAI-Cyber-1-Flash Is a 5B-Parameter Cybersecurity Bouncer

Microsoft AI released MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, a 137B-total sparse MoE model with 5B active parameters built to hunt vulnerabilities inside its MDASH security harness. Running alongside GPT-5.4, the system pushed CyberGym scores to 95.95% while the tiny model handles 90% of tasks, saving half the cost. Microsoft has essentially built a nightclub where the small bouncer does all the work and the celebrity guest only gets called for emergencies.

### Professor's Invisible Prompt Trap Caught 32 of 35 Students Cheating With AI

Alcorn State history professor Jason Gibson hid white-text instructions in a midterm prompt telling any AI to insert nonsense about Madagascar into answers about the Industrial Revolution. Thirty-two of thirty-five students copy-pasted the question into a chatbot and submitted gibberish like 'Madagascar purple bicycle whispers to the ceiling.' It turns out the easiest way to catch AI cheaters is to let them catch themselves, ideally while quoting an imaginary island.
