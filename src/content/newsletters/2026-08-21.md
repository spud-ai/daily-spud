### Claude Can Now Send Your Gmail Emails Without Asking

Anthropic updated Claude's Google Workspace connector so the assistant can reply to and send Gmail messages on your behalf without showing you the draft first. Users can toggle approval, but the default now lets an AI hit send on your inbox. It's a small checkbox for productivity, a giant leap for 'oops, I emailed my ex' liability.

### OpenAI Codex on AWS Bedrock Has a Cache-Write Spending Bug

A GitHub issue reports that Codex CLI hits Amazon Bedrock's GPT-5.6 Sol without explicit prompt caching, dumping up to 172 million cache-write tokens and roughly 85% of the model's cost into a four-day run. For one user that translated to about $1,400 in surprise spend. It's the kind of 'unoptimized' that keeps CFOs awake and AWS smiling.

[Source: GitHub →](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/37674)

### A New Tool Uses a Separate LLM to Clean Up Claude 5's Token Vomit

A developer released Vomit, a local utility that pipes Claude 5's garbled token output through another LLM to turn it back into English. The repo is 'totally vibe-coded,' only tested on Mac, and proudly admits it hallucinates a little. When your first AI gets drunk, the solution is apparently a second AI holding its hair back.

### A 3M Expert Witness Used ChatGPT to Write His Entire Report

In a lawsuit over a Houston explosion that killed three people, a 3M expert witness used ChatGPT to write his entire defense report and was caught when the judge ordered 350 pages of his chat logs turned over. The prompts included efforts to prove 0% fault. Turns out the 'expert' was just an autocomplete with a billing rate.
